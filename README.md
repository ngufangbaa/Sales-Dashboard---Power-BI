# Nestlé Australia Sales Performance Dashboard

## Overview

This project showcases an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyse Nestlé Australia's sales performance across products, regions, sales channels, and monthly trends. The objective was to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that support strategic business decisions and identify opportunities for growth.

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* Microsoft Excel
* Data Modelling
* DAX
* Data Visualisation

## Project Objectives

* Analyse product performance and revenue contribution.
* Evaluate sales across different channels.
* Identify seasonal sales trends.
* Compare regional performance.
* Provide data-driven recommendations to support business growth.

## Key Insights

### Product Performance

* Milo and Nescafé were the highest-performing products, each generating approximately **6 million** in revenue.
* KitKat recorded the lowest turnover at approximately **342,000**, indicating an opportunity for product and marketing optimisation.

### Sales Channels

* Direct sales generated approximately **24 million**, making it the strongest sales channel.
* Milo dominated direct sales, while Nescafé led online sales with approximately **3.09 million** in revenue.

### Seasonal Trends

* Sales peaked during **November and December**, highlighting the impact of festive demand and promotional campaigns.

### Regional Performance

* South Australia generated the highest revenue at approximately **5.5 million**.
* Western Australia recorded the lowest regional revenue, presenting an opportunity for targeted growth initiatives.

## Business Recommendations

* Strengthen marketing strategies for underperforming products such as KitKat.
* Continue investing in digital sales channels, particularly for high-performing products like Nescafé.
* Maximise festive campaigns during peak sales periods to increase revenue.
* Develop targeted regional marketing initiatives to improve performance in lower-performing areas.
* Continue investing in nutrition-focused product innovation to meet evolving consumer needs.
